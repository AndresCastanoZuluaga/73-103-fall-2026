73-103 · Principles of Macroeconomics

Professor: Andrés Castaño Zuluaga

Macroeconomics seeks to understand why some countries are rich and others poor; why some countries have high rates of inflation while others maintain stable prices; why countries experience phases of (sometimes rapid) expansion and subsequent (sometimes sharp) contraction; and how fiscal and monetary policy can reduce the frequency and severity of these episodes. Models you will actually use

We move from measurement to goods, financial markets, and IS–LM, then a thin labor / Phillips discussion, we transition into productivity and economic growth, and finish with the open economy.

Measurement

GDP, prices, employment: definition, limitations, and applications

Goods market

Aggregate demand, Multiplier, and Equilibrium

Financial markets

financial institutions, bond prices and yields, determination of the interest rate

IS–LM

Goods and financial markets together

Growth

Production functions and growth accounting

Labor markets, Phillips curve, and the open economy
